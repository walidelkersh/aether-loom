\sectionbar{1. Game overview}
\begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}p{0.27\linewidth}p{0.68\linewidth}}
Game & Aether Loom \\
Format & 5 reels × 3 rows; 243 ways \\
Bet & 0.20, 0.50, 1, 2, 5, or 10 fictional credits \\
Win direction & Consecutive reels from reel 1; longest occurrence per symbol \\
Original mechanic & Paid-spin outcomes move a persistent Tension state. The state selects the next physical reel set and becomes the bonus starting stage. \\
Bonus & Weave Ascending: 7 initial free spins, maximum 12; two or more Sparks advance the stage and add one spin. \\
Maximum intended win & 11,227.60× bet, exact reachable game-cycle maximum \\
Jackpot & None \\
\end{tabular}

\textbf{Intended experience.} Rest is the stable default. Two Sparks create visible anticipation by moving the loom to Taut, and a second two-Spark result reaches a brief Overdrive spin. The feature inherits earned tension and increases in volatility through richer Wild strips and multipliers of 1×, 1.75×, and 3.5×. The controlling parameters are the exact two-Spark transition probability, state-specific strip composition, and stage multiplier schedule.

\sectionbar{2. Symbols and paytable}
All pays are multiples of total bet. Wild substitutes for all six regular symbols, has no direct pay, and never appears on reel 1. Spark has no cash pay.

For each paying symbol, matching instances on a reel include that symbol and Wild. Ways equal the product of the matching counts across the consecutive reels. Different symbol wins are added. Reel 1 contains no Wild, preventing an all-Wild starting ambiguity.

\sectionbar{3. Reel architecture and restrictions}
There are six named reel sets. Every reel is a circular ordered strip with 40 stops. The uniformly sampled stop is the top visible symbol and the following two positions are the middle and bottom. Wild and Spark occurrences are separated by at least three circular positions, so a three-row window contains at most one of each special per reel.

\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{l l c l}

\sectionbar{4. Persistent Tension mathematics}
After a paid spin: 3+ Sparks trigger the feature and reset to Rest afterward; an Overdrive paid spin vents to Rest; exactly 2 Sparks move Rest→Taut or Taut→Overdrive; 0 Sparks lower tension one step; 1 Spark holds.

\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{l r r r}
\hline Current & Next Rest & Next Taut & Next Overdrive\\\hline
Rest & 95.5481\% & 4.4519\% & 0.0000\%\\
Taut & 58.0910\% & 34.0913\% & 7.8177\%\\
Overdrive & 100.0000\% & 0.0000\% & 0.0000\%\\\hline
Stationary mix & 93.2116\% & 6.2962\% & 0.4922\%\\\hline
\end{tabular}\end{center}

The transition matrix comes from exact scatter-count polynomials for each active strip set. The stationary vector weights base EV and state-conditioned feature entry in the long-run model.

\sectionbar{5. Weave Ascending feature mathematics}
The trigger is 3+ Sparks on a paid spin. The feature inherits the triggering paid-spin state, begins with seven spins, and ends after the queue is empty or 12 spins have played. On each feature spin, 2+ Sparks add one spin and advance one stage, capped at Overdrive.

\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{l r r r}
\hline Starting stage & Multiplier & Exact expected award & Exact expected spins\\\hline
Rest & 1.00× & 32.431583× & 8.370532\\
Taut & 1.75× & 61.467090× & 8.370532\\
Overdrive & 3.50× & 96.017658× & 8.370532\\\hline
Trigger-weighted & -- & 38.360288× & 8.370532\\\hline
\end{tabular}\end{center}

The feature is a finite Markov reward process indexed by current stage, spins remaining, and spins played. Exact stage EV is added to the probability-weighted next-state values. This recursion terminates at the 12-spin bound.
